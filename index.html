Glossary

Barangay — Smallest administrative division in the Philippines; the local government unit a subdivision or condo falls under.
BIR — Bureau of Internal Revenue. PH tax authority; HOAs file annual returns.
DPA — Data Privacy Act (RA 10173). Philippine law governing personal data collection, processing, and storage.
DPO — Data Protection Officer. Person designated to oversee DPA compliance within an organization.
DSR — Data Subject Request. A resident's formal request to access, correct, or erase their personal data.
Edge server — On-premise device at the gate that runs offline when internet is down; syncs with cloud when connected.
EV — Electric Vehicle.
GCash / Maya — Leading Philippine e-wallet payment platforms. Most common digital payment methods for HOA dues.
HOA — Homeowners Association. The governing body of a subdivision, village, or condo; the buyer of AccessGates.
Lien — Legal claim filed against a property for unpaid HOA dues. Last resort in collections escalation.
NFC — Near-Field Communication. Short-range wireless tech used in guard patrol checkpoints and some access cards.
NPC — National Privacy Commission. PH government agency that enforces the Data Privacy Act.
Org — Organization. One HOA or condo association in AccessGates — the tenant boundary for multi-tenancy.
P&L — Profit and Loss statement. Financial report showing income vs expenses over a period.
PDC — Post-Dated Check. Common PH payment method where residents issue checks dated for future months.
RA 10173 — Republic Act 10173 — the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.
RFID — Radio-Frequency Identification. Wireless tech used in gate access cards and vehicle stickers.
RLS — Row-Level Security. Postgres feature ensuring one org's data is invisible to another org's queries.
RoPA — Record of Processing Activities. DPA-required register of what data is collected, why, and how long it's kept.
SLA — Service Level Agreement. Target response/resolution times for complaints or support requests.
SOA — Statement of Account. Monthly billing summary sent to each unit showing charges, payments, and balance.
Sinking fund — Reserve fund collected from dues for major future expenses (roof repair, elevator replacement, repainting).
Walk-up — Unregistered visitor arriving at the gate without a pre-made pass; guard initiates real-time approval from resident.
